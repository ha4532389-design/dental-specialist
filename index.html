About the Practice

Surgical precision, delivered with genuine warmth.

Dr. Naureen Sarwar is an oral and dental surgeon based in Wapda Town, Lahore, and an Associate Professor at de'Montmorency College of Dentistry. Her clinical work spans surgical extractions, root canal treatment, crowns and bridges, and gum care — grounded in postgraduate training in oral surgery and restorative dentistry.

Teaching keeps the practice current: every plan is explained in plain language, every option is discussed before treatment begins, and every appointment is unhurried. Patients are seen by the same surgeon from first consultation through to follow-up.
